## Reading

What the numbers say, and how much of it you are actually claiming. "Submissions doubled, and we
shipped the form inside that window" is supportable. "We doubled submissions" is a causal claim
that a before and an after cannot carry on their own.

Name the share you attribute to the work, and say what you would need to separate it properly.

month, whether the effect survives the campaign ending.
