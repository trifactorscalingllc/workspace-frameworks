## Reading

12 to 25 on the same metric, same unit, measured the same way. The form shipped 2026-08-09,
inside the window.

How much we claim: not all of it. The paid campaign overlaps three of the four weeks, and 4 of
the 25 came through a referral partner who would have converted anyway. Stripping those leaves
21 organic against a 12 baseline in a month that was itself a seasonal low. A fair statement is
that the form is associated with a meaningful rise and none of the evidence contradicts it.
Separating campaign from form properly needs a week with the campaign paused.

## What this does not show

Whether the submissions are better quality, and whether it holds once the campaign ends.
