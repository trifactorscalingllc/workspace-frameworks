## Claim

> The exact words, if you are quoting.

Multiple distinct claims from one source are fine — number them. What does not belong here is any
word explaining, weighing or reacting to the claim. That is Analysis.

## Context

Optional. Who wrote it, when, and anything about provenance that bears on how much weight it can
carry — funding, sample size, whether it is reporting its own product.
